Hong Kong Hiking Guide

About Us

Practical route guides, hiking gear advice and safer trail planning for Hong Kong.

Home / About Us

Hong Kong Hiking Guide was created to make hiking information in Hong Kong easier to understand and use. Our goal is to help more people discover local trails, prepare with suitable equipment and make safer decisions before heading outdoors.

What we hope to achieve

Hong Kong has a wide variety of hiking routes, from short, accessible walks to challenging mountain trails. However, people who are new to hiking may find it difficult to decide where to go, what to bring and how to prepare for changing weather and trail conditions.

We want to bring useful information together in one place. By presenting route introductions, gear advice and practical hiking tips in clear language, we hope to make planning a hike less confusing and help readers feel more prepared.

Who this website is for

New hikers

Start with confidence

Understand basic hiking preparation, choose a suitable beginner-friendly route and learn what to bring before your first hike.

Regular hikers

Plan your next trail

Explore Hong Kong hiking destinations, compare route information and find practical reminders for different trail conditions.

Outdoor learners

Build better habits

Learn about clothing, footwear, backpacks, food, water, navigation and responsible behaviour in the countryside.

What you can find here

  • Hiking destinations: introductions to trails in Hong Kong, with route-planning information to help readers compare different options.
  • Hiking gear: practical guidance on choosing clothing, shoes, backpacks, food and water for a day hike.
  • Hiking tips: preparation checklists, beginner advice, weather awareness, safety reminders and navigation tips.

How we approach our content

We aim to make our guides practical, clear and responsible. When preparing information, we focus on the details that can help readers plan ahead and understand the demands of a hike.

  • Useful information: explain route characteristics, preparation steps and gear considerations in straightforward language.
  • Reliable references: use relevant official information, including guidance from the Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department and the Hong Kong Observatory, where appropriate.
  • Safety awareness: remind readers to check the latest weather, warnings and trail conditions before setting out.
  • Responsible hiking: encourage visitors to respect nature, follow marked trails and take their rubbish home.
  • Continuous improvement: review our content and make corrections when information needs updating.

Our commitment

Our aim is not simply to list places to visit. We hope this website can be a useful starting point for anyone interested in exploring Hong Kong on foot. Whether you are preparing for your first short walk or looking for ideas for your next trail, we want to help you make informed choices and enjoy the outdoors responsibly.

Have a suggestion or noticed information that may need updating? Get in touch with us. Your feedback can help us make this guide more useful for the hiking community.
