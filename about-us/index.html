About Us

Hong Kong Hiking Guide is a student-created resource designed to help readers explore Hong Kong's countryside with better preparation and confidence.

What this guide covers

Our website brings together practical information about hiking destinations, route planning, essential gear and hiking safety.

  • Trail guides for selected hiking destinations in Hong Kong
  • Guidance on clothing, footwear, backpacks, food and water
  • Practical tips on preparation, navigation, weather and safety

Our aim

We aim to make hiking information easier to understand, especially for people who are starting to explore Hong Kong's trails. Always check official trail notices and weather information before setting out.
