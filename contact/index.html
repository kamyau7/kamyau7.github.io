Information on this website

Hong Kong Hiking Guide shares general information to help readers explore local trails, prepare hiking gear and plan more safely. You may find the following sections useful:

  • Route planning: visit our Hong Kong hiking guide for general planning information.
  • Trail comparisons: use the best hiking trails guide to explore route options.
  • Gear advice: read our hiking gear guide for clothing, footwear, backpacks, food and water.
  • Preparation and safety: browse our beginner hiking tips and hiking safety guide.

Emergency assistance

This website is not an emergency service and cannot provide real-time assistance. If you are in danger or need emergency help in Hong Kong, call 999. Always check the latest official weather warnings, trail notices and transport information before setting out.

A note about our information

Trail conditions, facilities, public transport services and safety advice may change. Use this website as a starting point for planning, and confirm important details with the relevant official authorities and service operators before your hike.
