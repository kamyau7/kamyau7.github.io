Contact Us

Trail questions, corrections and route reports all land in the same inbox. We read everything and reply to most things within two working days.

Get in touch

Email: hello@ridgeandcoast.hk — replace this address with your group's real contact address before publishing.

For corrections, please include the page URL and the specific detail that is wrong. A photo of the trail sign helps more than a description.

Insert your contact form here once the site is live. WordPress does not include one by default — install either WPForms Lite or Contact Form 7 from the plugin directory, then paste the shortcode into a Shortcode block in this space.

What we can help with

  • Choosing a route that matches your fitness, your group and the hours you have.
  • Transport questions — which bus, which stop, how late the last one runs.
  • Corrections to any trail, transport or safety detail on the site.
  • Permission requests for using our photographs or text.

What we cannot help with

  • Emergency assistance. If you are in trouble on a trail, call 999 immediately.
  • Guided hikes or transport bookings — we do not run trips or take commissions.
  • Personal injury or medical advice.

Response times

We are a five-person team writing in our own time, so expect a reply within two working days. Corrections about safety information are treated as urgent and usually fixed the same day.

Before you write

Most questions we receive are already answered somewhere on the site. If you are new to hiking in Hong Kong, the beginner's guide covers seasons, water, transport and safety. If you are choosing a route, the trail index compares all eight by distance, climb and grade.

For anything about how we research and check routes, see about us.
