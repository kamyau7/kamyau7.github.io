This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information may be processed when you visit Hong Kong Hiking Guide and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 28 November 2026.

Who we are

Hong Kong Hiking Guide is an informational website about hiking routes, equipment and preparation in Hong Kong. The website is published as a static website using GitHub Pages. It does not offer user accounts, online purchases or a public contact form.

Information processed when you visit

Website hosting and delivery. When a browser requests a webpage, the hosting and delivery services needed to provide that page may process technical information associated with the request. We do not operate a separate visitor registration system or ask visitors to submit personal information to read the website. For details about information handled by the hosting provider, please refer to GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Website analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID: G-MC61HQL7KT) to understand general use of the website, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors interact with the site. Depending on browser settings and the analytics configuration, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and process information such as page addresses, device/browser information, approximate location and online identifiers. We do not use this website to ask visitors for sensitive personal information.

Google Analytics and third-party processing

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Information collected through the analytics tag may be transmitted to and processed by Google in accordance with Google’s terms and privacy practices. We do not control Google’s independent processing. For more information, please read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Analytics’ information about safeguarding data.

Cookies and your choices

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to help measure website usage. Their availability and behaviour can depend on your browser, device and applicable settings. You can review or restrict cookies through your browser settings. If you block analytics-related cookies or technologies, some analytics measurements may not be recorded. The website’s informational pages remain available.

Contact and information requests

This website does not currently provide a public email address or an online contact form. We therefore cannot receive privacy requests or individual enquiries through the website at this time. Please do not submit personal or confidential information through public website links or page content.

Data retention

Analytics data retention is controlled in the Google Analytics property settings. We have not stated a specific retention period here because that setting has not been independently verified for this website. Technical records associated with hosting and delivery are handled under the relevant service provider’s policies and retention practices.

Children and sensitive information

This website is a general informational resource and is not designed to request personal information from children. Please do not post or send sensitive personal information through the website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the website or its data practices change. The “Last updated” date above indicates when this policy was most recently revised. Please review this page periodically.

Further information

For information about data protection in Hong Kong, you may consult the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data.
