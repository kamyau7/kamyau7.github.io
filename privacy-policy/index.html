Home / Privacy Policy

This page explains what data Ridge & Coast collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

Who we are

Ridge & Coast is an independent hiking guide covering the country parks of Hong Kong. We do not sell products, run a shop, or collect payment details. The site is funded by our own time.

What we collect

We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run and improve the site. In practice that means three things.

  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are read, how visitors arrive, and which devices they use. Google Analytics records an anonymised identifier, approximate location (city level), device type and pages viewed. We have not enabled advertising features or data sharing for advertising.
  • Contact form data. If you email us or use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and whatever you write. We keep it only as long as needed to answer you.
  • Cookies. Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish visitors. WordPress may set a cookie if you log in to the site; ordinary visitors are not logged in and do not receive one.

Why we are allowed to collect it

Under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, we collect analytics data on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used, and contact data because you chose to send it to us. We do not use your data for marketing, and we do not build profiles of individual readers.

Who else sees it

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) processes visit data on our behalf. Google may transfer this data outside Hong Kong; its privacy terms apply.
  • Our hosting provider stores the site and, like all web servers, logs IP addresses for security and troubleshooting.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data to anyone.

How long we keep it

Analytics data is retained for 14 months and then deleted automatically. Emails and contact form messages are deleted once the conversation is finished, and in any case within twelve months.

Your choices

  • You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings without losing access to any content on this site.
  • You can ask us what data we hold about you, request a copy, or ask us to delete it by emailing the address on our contact page.
  • If you believe we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data in Hong Kong.

Editorial independence and affiliate links

No brand or tour operator pays to appear on this site, and no ranking is influenced by payment. If we ever add affiliate links to gear, we will mark them clearly in the text and on this page. At the time of writing, the site carries no affiliate links and no advertising.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in a site update post.
